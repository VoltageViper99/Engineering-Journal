## Work completed

- Continued the internal reverse proxy rollout.
- Moved the media stack behind Caddy, including qBittorrent.
- Moved the AdGuard admin UI to port 3000 and removed obsolete firewall rules that were only there for its old port.
- Removed Homarr, since Control Centre now replaces it.
- Updated Control Centre links to point at the new service names.

## Architecture / decisions

- Wildcard DNS for `*.voltinfra.net` points clients at the proxy.
- Internal services are intended to be reachable over LAN and Tailscale.
- Caddy handles internal reverse proxying.

## Why

- Reaching services by name is easier to remember and maintain than addresses and ports.
- It reduces the number of host ports exposed directly.
- One dashboard is easier to keep correct than two.

## What I learned

- Cleaning up old firewall rules is part of a migration. Leftover rules for retired ports are clutter and extra exposure.
