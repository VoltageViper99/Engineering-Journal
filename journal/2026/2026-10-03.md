## What I worked on

Continuing the internal reverse proxy rollout (pattern described in [Internal Reverse Proxy](../../infrastructure/reverse-proxy.md)), plus some cleanup and an incident.

## What changed

- Moved the media stack, including its download client, behind Caddy.
- Moved the DNS filter's admin UI to a different port and removed the firewall rules that only existed for its old one.
- Removed Homarr, since the [Control Centre](../../projects/control-centre.md) now replaces it.
- Updated Control Centre links to the new service names.
- Replaced a signing key that had been committed to version control and blocked that class of file from the repository. See [the case study](../../security/incident-response.md).

## Decisions

- Wildcard DNS for an internal domain points clients at the proxy.
- Internal services are intended to be reachable over LAN and Tailscale.
- Caddy handles internal reverse proxying.

Why: names are easier to remember and maintain than addresses and ports, the number of directly exposed host ports drops, and one dashboard is easier to keep correct than two.

## What I learned

- Cleaning up old firewall rules is part of a migration. Leftover rules for retired ports are clutter and extra exposure.

## Next steps

- Add the remaining media tooling.
- Repoint Uptime Kuma monitors at the new service names.
- Narrow media host-port exposure where it is safe to do so.
