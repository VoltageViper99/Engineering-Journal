> [!NOTE]
> **In short:** I let the server update some programs on its own, but only the low-risk ones, like the media tools and monitoring. Anything holding family files, photos, home automation or business records still waits for me to update it by hand, because a bad update there would hurt far more. New programs start out manual until I decide otherwise.

New to some of the terms? The [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) explains them in plain English.

## What changed

- Enabled automatic updates, restricted to containers explicitly opted in by label.
- Opted in low-risk services such as media tooling, DNS filtering, monitoring and notification services.
- Left family file storage, photo backup, home automation and the business pipeline on manual updates.

## Decisions

Automatic updates close security gaps quickly, but a mismatched app and database version can break a service. The opt-in label keeps that choice per service.

## What I learned

- "Update automatically" should be a per-service decision based on blast radius.
- Opt-in is safer than opt-out: new services stay manual until I decide otherwise.

## Next steps

- Check update notifications as part of regular maintenance.
